# JEDeye landing page — design context

## Design Context

### Users
Investors reading on a laptop after seeing the pitch deck. They already know the story;
they come here to check that the technology is real, current and measurable. Secondary:
hospital leaders and partners forwarded the link. Gated by a shared-password login.

### Brand Personality
Clinical, precise, evidenced. Three words: **calm, exact, accountable**.
Emotional goal: "these people measure everything" — confidence earned by numbers and
real footage, never by hype.

### Aesthetic Direction
Light theme. Paper-white surfaces tinted toward the brand teal, one teal accent used
sparingly for data and actions, ink-dark text. Real model outputs (segmentation,
phase timelines, remaining-time curves, 3D splats) are the imagery; no stock OR photos,
no glassmorphism, no glow, no gradient text, no hero video.
Anti-references: the previous site (dark glass cards over a blurred OR photo, 19 MB
scroll-scrubbed video), generic SaaS launch pages.

### Design Principles
1. Evidence first — every claim sits next to a number, a figure or a clip that backs it.
2. Lightweight by construction — media budget ~5 MB total for the first screenful, lazy
   everything else, no video over 3 MB, prefer webp/avif stills and short webm loops.
3. One accent, rarely — teal marks data and primary actions only.
4. Interactive means inspectable — sliders, scrubbers and toggles let the reader probe
   real outputs, not decorative motion.
5. Keep the business sections, but shorter and typographic — no icon-card grids.
